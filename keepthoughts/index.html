KeepThoughts

A simple and private space to capture your ideas, notes, memories, and daily thoughts.

KeepThoughts keeps writing things down uncomplicated. Open it, write, and move on — no accounts to create, no setup to work through, and no clutter between you and a blank page.

Your notes are stored on your device, and — with Premium's iCloud sync — in your own iCloud account, so they follow you across your devices. Either way they stay within Apple's ecosystem: they are never collected, accessed, or transmitted to the developer.

Privacy

Your notes are yours.

On your device Never sold Your iCloud

The notes, thoughts, and journal entries you create are stored on your device. With Premium's iCloud sync they are also stored in your own iCloud account and handled by Apple under its terms. Their content is never collected, accessed, or received by the developer, and is never sold or shared with third parties.

KeepThoughts is free and supported by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, IP address, advertising ID, app usage metrics, and diagnostic information in order to deliver and measure ads. That data is handled by Google under its own privacy practices.
