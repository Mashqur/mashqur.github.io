HeadRoom for macOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 August 2026

HeadRoom is a macOS storage analyzer and cleanup utility. It is designed to work entirely on your Mac. HeadRoom does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.

What HeadRoom does not do

  • It has no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind.
  • It has no user accounts and never asks you to sign in.
  • It does not upload your files, file names, folder paths, or storage figures anywhere.
  • It does not contain third-party advertising or data-sharing SDKs.
  • The developer receives no information about what is on your Mac.

What stays on your Mac

To do its job, HeadRoom reads file sizes, modification dates, and folder names in the locations you allow it to inspect. That information is used to display the scan results and is never sent off the device. HeadRoom also stores a small amount of data locally in standard macOS app preferences:

  • Which folders you have granted access to, so you are not asked repeatedly.
  • Whether the one free cleanup has been used.
  • Your chosen scan profile and window layout.

This data lives inside HeadRoom's own application container and is removed when you delete the app.

Folder access

HeadRoom runs in Apple's App Sandbox. It can inspect your Downloads folder and any additional folders you explicitly choose from a system file picker. It has no access to anything else, and you can revoke any folder permission from inside the app at any time.

Purchases

HeadRoom Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Payments, subscription management, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are handled entirely by Apple. HeadRoom asks Apple whether the current Apple Account holds a valid purchase and receives only a yes-or-no answer. The developer never sees your name, email address, payment details, or Apple Account information.

Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

HeadRoom is a general-purpose utility, is not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date above.
