macOS App

HeadRoom

A local-first storage analyzer and cautious cleanup utility for the Mac.

HeadRoom shows you where your disk space actually went, then helps you reclaim it without guessing. It explains every item before anything is removed: how large it is, what created it, whether it rebuilds itself, and exactly what will happen if you clean it.

It is built for two kinds of people. Everyday Mac users who want their Downloads folder under control, and developers whose disks quietly fill with build artifacts, simulator runtimes, container storage, and package caches.

Nothing is deleted without confirmation, and nothing leaves your Mac.

What it does

Built to be careful first.

01

Analysis

See where the space went

Scan profiles for everyday use, development work, containers and virtual machines, and creative media. Each result shows its true size on disk, when it was last touched, and its exact path.

02

Safety

Every item is explained

Items are labelled by what removing them costs you: safe, rebuilds itself, personal file, or application state. Personal files are never selected for you, and protected locations are never offered at all.

03

Control

Trash or delete, your choice

Every cleanup asks first, and lets you choose per run: move items to the Trash so they stay recoverable, or delete them permanently to reclaim the space immediately.

04

Developer tools

The folders that quietly grow

Derived data, simulator runtimes and devices, container storage, virtual machine bundles, and package caches for npm, pip, Gradle, Go, Cargo and more — reported clearly, with rebuild cost stated up front.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your Mac.

No accounts No analytics No tracking

HeadRoom has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no user accounts. It never uploads your files, file names, folder paths, or storage figures anywhere. The developer receives no information about what is on your Mac.

It runs inside Apple's App Sandbox and can only inspect your Downloads folder plus folders you explicitly choose. Every permission can be revoked from inside the app.
